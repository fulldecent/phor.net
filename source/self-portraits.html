Detailed, third person (~90 words) — default host script

William Entriken is the lead author of ERC-721, the Ethereum non-fungible token standard. He works as an independent advisor on tokenization, enterprise blockchain delivery, and regulated implementations. In 2019 he was an independent contractor to EY’s Global Innovation Blockchain R&D center on the token design behind Nightfall. He has served on Arianee’s advisory board, done product design for Namefi and for Genobank.io laboratory-result certificates, and reviewed smart contracts for production marketplaces including Treasure. He also operates Modern Training Solutions, a remote professional-education platform with company-published figures of 150,000+ enrollments and statewide Pennsylvania training contracts at 98%+ completion.
